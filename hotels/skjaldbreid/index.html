Historic Reykjavík hotel with charm

Skjaldbreið is set in one of Reykjavik’s oldest and most striking buildings on Laugavegur. With its small size and warm hospitality, this Reykjavík hotel makes every guest feel at home while offering easy access to downtown Reykjavik.

1998

The buildingThebuilding

Originally a pharmacy, where the pharmacist lived, worked and made medicines

Four years later, we bought our first hotel: Skjaldbreið on Laugavegur, Reykjavík’s main shopping street. The stately building was originally a pharmacy, where the pharmacist lived, worked and made medicines. Turning it into a 33-room hotel took serious work. While removing a broken window, Kristófer nearly cut off his hand. Luckily, Svanfríður knew a specialist nerve doctor, who performed groundbreaking surgery on his arm. Kristófer was back at work the next day. We really did work our fingers to the bone to open Skjaldbreið. Literally.
