Boutique hotel in Reykjavík

Þingholt is a boutique hotel in central Reykjavik, located just off Laugavegur, the city’s main shopping street. With an intimate atmosphere, spa facilities, and a cozy bar, it’s a great choice for travelers looking for charm and comfort in the heart of the city.

2006

The buildingThebuilding

The Ísafold printing house, 1942 to 1994

Hotel number three came with a story we couldn’t resist. From 1942 to 1994, the building was the Ísafold printing house, which also printed Morgunblaðið, Iceland’s national newspaper. The building sits on Þingholtsstræti, one of the oldest streets in the city center. Instead of following the style of our first two hotels, we did something new and made Þingholt a boutique hotel. It has 52 stylish rooms, each with its own identity.
