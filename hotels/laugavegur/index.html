Hotel on Reykjavík’s main street

Right on Laugavegur, Reykjavík’s famous shopping street, this hotel captures the city’s quirky, urban vibe. Perfect for exploring downtown Reykjavík, Center Hotels Laugavegur is one of the best hotels in Reykjavík for travelers who want shopping, dining, and nightlife just steps away.

2019

The buildingThebuilding

The first hotel in the refreshed Center Hotels look

Laugavegur roughly translates as “Pool Road”. It was the old route to the hot springs where Reykjavík locals once did their laundry. Today it’s the city’s main shopping street and home to our seventh hotel, with 102 rooms and a restaurant. At 25 years, we refreshed the Center Hotels brand, and Laugavegur was the first hotel in our new look. It’s full of stories, quotes and sayings about Iceland and Icelanders, so our guests can feel part of the family.
